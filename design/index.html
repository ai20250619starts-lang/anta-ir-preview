IR overview
  1. 01

    Leader in China

    An estimated ~21.8% share of China’s sportswear market in 2025, per an internationally recognised institution cited by ANTA, built on a “Brand + Retail” model and over 800 supply-chain partners.

    Source (opens external site)

    ~21.8%China sportswear market share, 2025

  2. 02

    Multi-brand platform

    Six in-house brands — ANTA, FILA, DESCENTE, KOLON SPORT, JACK WOLFSKIN and MAIA ACTIVE — spanning mass to premium segments, complemented by strategic investments in Amer Sports and MUSINSA.

    Source (opens external site)

    6 + 2In-house brands + strategic investments

  3. 03

    Efficient management

    Operating profit margin up 0.7 ppt to 27.0% and gross profit margin up 0.5 ppt to 63.9% in the first half of 2026.

    Source (opens external site)

    27.0%Operating profit margin, 1H26 (+0.7 ppt)

    Global expansion

    Largest shareholder of NYSE-listed Amer Sports (Arc’teryx, Salomon, Wilson); completed the JACK WOLFSKIN acquisition in 2025; ranked among the industry’s global top three.

    Source (opens external site)

    Top 3Ranked among the industry’s global top three

    Shareholder returns
