# ANTA Sports IR home page: visual concept "Lane Lines"

The static mockup is `design/index.html`. It has no build step and uses only local fonts and images. It is generated by `design/src/build.py` from the repo's own data files: `quote.mock.json` and `financial-highlights.json`. Screenshots are in `design/screenshots/`. Only files under `design/` were added. No existing site source was changed.

## 1. The idea

**A running track, used as a grid.** Fine hairline "lanes" give the page its structure. The ANTA red works like the lead runner: it shows up **once per view** as a single lane, rule or marker, and never as a large fill. In the hero, nine lanes curve around a track bend, and one red lane runs along the bend and into the red top edge of the *Latest results* card. That makes the brand idea "Keep Moving" into the way the page reads: from positioning, to the share price, to the newest results.

The typography is set like an editorial page. Large, tightly set Inter headlines sit next to Barlow Condensed figures, so every number gets the visual weight of a sports scoreboard. The surfaces are near-black ink, white and a cool grey. That keeps the page calm, institutional and easy to read in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

**Why this suits an HK-listed global sportswear group's IR audience.** Analysts and fund managers in HK, Mainland China and overseas come for three things, in this order: the share price, the latest results and the investment case. The hero puts the first two in front of them straight away, in the first viewport on desktop. The scoreboard-style figures and the lane motif show that this is a sports company without using lifestyle photography, which is harder to keep current. Holding the red back keeps the site trustworthy and leaves the logo free to carry the brand.

**How it differs from the current preview.** The current preview uses a warm paper background, outlined boxes and a vertical results list, and every section has similar weight. The new concept changes that in five ways:
- a dark, confident hero with the quote panel built into it
- a horizontal results band that overlaps the hero
- a Why Invest section where each point leads with its key figure
- KPI tiles with six-year trend bars
- strategic investments shown separately, with their reason given in words

**No new Latin fonts are needed.** The concept reuses the repo's Inter and Barlow Condensed and gets its fresh look from layout and scale.

**Recommended CJK strategy**, using the same `:lang()` stacks as the mockup:
```
:lang(zh-Hant) "Inter Variable", "Noto Sans TC Sub", "PingFang HK", "PingFang TC", "Microsoft JhengHei", "Noto Sans CJK TC", "Source Han Sans TC", sans-serif
:lang(zh-Hans) "Inter Variable", "Noto Sans SC Sub", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", "Noto Sans CJK SC", "Source Han Sans SC", sans-serif
```
1. **Body text and dynamic content** (announcement titles, which change daily) use the **system CJK fonts**. This costs 0 KB. PingFang on Apple devices and JhengHei/YaHei on Windows are high quality.
2. **Static UI and headings** use a **build-time subset** of Noto Sans TC/SC, generated from `ui.ts`, `nav.ts` and `why-invest.ts`. That gives brand-consistent headings on every OS. I measured it: **484 glyphs ≈ 134 KB per weight** (woff2). Ship 700 only, plus 400 if needed. Preload only on TC or SC pages.
3. **Avoid shipping full fonts.** I subset every CJK character in the repo's data: 2,075 glyphs came to about 605 KB per weight. A full Noto Sans TC weight is several MB, so full fonts are not practical. The alternative is `@fontsource/noto-sans-tc`, which uses about 100 `unicode-range` slices so a page downloads only what it uses. Typically that is a few hundred KB, but it means many requests.

Inter comes first in each stack so that Latin letters and digits inside Chinese text stay consistent with the English site.

### Spacing, grid, radii, shadows
- **Spacing scale (px):** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 112. Section padding is 72 on mobile and 112 on desktop. Space below a section head is 28 on mobile and 44 on desktop.
- **Container:** 1280 px max. Side gutter is 20 px on mobile, 32 px from 768 and 40 px from 1280.
- **Grid:** 12 columns from 1024. Hero 7/5. Why Invest 4/8. Events 7/5. Footer: brand row, then 6 nav columns.
- **Breakpoints:** sm 640, md 768, lg 1024, `nav` 1100 (the full desktop navigation appears here), xl 1280.
- **Radii:** `sm` 4 (tags, badges), `md` 8 (icon wells, calendar tiles), `lg` 12 (cards and tiles), `pill` 999 (buttons, toggles, language switcher).
- **Shadows:** `card` = `0 1px 2px rgb(14 15 17/.06), 0 1px 1px rgb(14 15 17/.04)`. `raised` = `0 12px 32px -12px rgb(14 15 17/.18), 0 2px 6px rgb(14 15 17/.06)`. Use `raised` for the quote card, the results band and hovered brand tiles.

### Motion
- On load, the red hero lane draws itself (stroke-dashoffset, 1.6 s, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`), and the hero copy and quote card rise 12 px (0.7 s).
- Hover effects take 150 to 200 ms: arrow nudge, tile lift, icon well inversion.
- No parallax, no auto-rotating carousels and no number count-ups on financial figures. Count-ups could make figures look like they are changing.
- `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` switches off every animation and transition and shows the lane fully drawn. This is already in the CSS.

### Accessibility notes
- Direction is never shown by colour alone. Every change has an ▲/▼ glyph, a +/− sign (using the true minus sign U+2212), the % or ppt unit, and an sr-only sentence.
- The "Illustrative" label and the 15-minute delay are shown as text on the quote card. They are not tooltips.
- Landmarks: skip link, `header`, `nav[aria-label=Main]`, `main`, `footer` with `nav[aria-label=Footer]`. There is one H1, each section has its own H2, and each section is labelled with `aria-labelledby`.
- Focus: a 3 px `#FF0000` outline with 3 px offset. It measures 4.0:1 on white and 4.8:1 on ink, which passes the 3:1 non-text requirement.
- Touch targets are at least 44 px for buttons, links in lists and the menu. Footer links are 32 px high with spacing.
- Set `lang` on every TC/SC fragment (`zh-Hant-HK` / `zh-Hans-CN`) so screen readers and font stacks switch correctly. The language links carry `lang` and `title`.
- External links use a ↗ icon and sr-only "(opens external site)".
- Charts are `aria-hidden` and their values are provided as text.

## 5. Open questions for the client
